History

Founded in 1952 as the team of Tash-Kömür's coal miners, the club took the name Naryn in 1993. It is owned by the Naryn cascade power stations, and its crest shows an arch dam of the cascade. It has not yet won the Irad League.

Irad League

The Irad League (Irad Ligasy) is the top division of club football in the Irad Special Economic Zone, run by the Football Federation of Irad (Irad Futbol Federatsiyasy), founded in Talas in 1993.

Broadcast partner: IradOne Sport. Russian commentary of Neftchi matches on IradOdin.
