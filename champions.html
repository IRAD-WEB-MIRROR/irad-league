Champions

Neftchi Nurzhan hold the record of 13 titles, ahead of Tulpar Talas with 11; between them the two clubs have won 24 of the 31 championships. The 2007 season was cancelled because of the war.

Format

  • 1994–2019: Eight clubs, four rounds (28 matchdays).
  • 2020–: Ten clubs, three rounds (27 matchdays), March to November.
Irad League

The Irad League (Irad Ligasy) is the top division of club football in the Irad Special Economic Zone, run by the Football Federation of Irad (Irad Futbol Federatsiyasy), founded in Talas in 1993.

Broadcast partner: IradOne Sport. Russian commentary of Neftchi matches on IradOdin.
