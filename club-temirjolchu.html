History

Founded in 1936 as Lokomotiv Kobozev, the railway workers' club played mainly in the republican championship and cup of the Soviet period. Under the federation's 1993 naming rules it became Temirjolchu Nurzhan.

Owned by Irad Temir Jolu, the club plays at the Lokomotiv Stadium by Nurzhan station and has won the Irad League three times.

Irad League

The Irad League (Irad Ligasy) is the top division of club football in the Irad Special Economic Zone, run by the Football Federation of Irad (Irad Futbol Federatsiyasy), founded in Talas in 1993.

Broadcast partner: IradOne Sport. Russian commentary of Neftchi matches on IradOdin.
