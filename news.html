Saturday 31 October 2026Irad League

Neftchi Nurzhan are the 2026 Irad League champions

Neftchi Nurzhan beat Tulpar Talas 2–1 at NurGaz Stadium in the last round and finish one point clear, 63 to 62. It is the club's fourteenth title. Naryn Tash-Kömür are relegated.

Neftchi Nurzhan are the champions of the 2026 Irad League. In the 27th and last round on Saturday 31 October, Neftchi beat Tulpar Talas 2–1 in the Kara-Ak derby at a sold-out NurGaz Stadium and finished the season on 63 points, one ahead of Tulpar on 62.

It is Neftchi's fourteenth league title and their second in a row. Tulpar Talas finish as runners-up. Dastgoh Nurzhan are third on 42 points.

Naryn Tash-Kömür finish last on 8 points and are relegated to the First League. Their place is taken by the winner of the First League, decided in the last round on Sunday 1 November.

The trophy will be presented to Neftchi Nurzhan at a date to be announced by the Federation.

Thursday 22 October 2026Federation

FFI to seek to host an international futsal championship in 2030

The federation will put Irad forward to host an international futsal championship for CONIFA and invited federations at the Irad Central Sports Complex in Talas.

The Football Federation of Irad said on Thursday that it will seek to host an international futsal championship in 2030. The tournament would be open to CONIFA members and invited federations and would be played at the Irad Central Sports Complex in Talas, home of the national futsal cup.

Irad cannot take part in bidding for the European futsal championship, because the federation is not a member of a recognised confederation and FIFA membership requires a recognised state. FFI president Kanat Ermekov presented the plan as a sporting ambition that does not depend on recognition.

This is an expression of interest. No host has been chosen, and no budget or dates have been fixed.

Tuesday 20 October 2026Irad League

Kara-Ak derby on 31 October: kick-off, tickets and stadium rules

Neftchi Nurzhan v Tulpar Talas, the final round of the 2026 Irad League, kicks off at 18:00 at the NurGaz Stadium. Tickets are on sale at the grounds.

The Kara-Ak derby between Neftchi Nurzhan and Tulpar Talas closes the 2026 Irad League on Saturday 31 October at the NurGaz Stadium, Nurzhan. Kick-off is at 18:00 Irad Time; the other four matches of round 27 kick off at 17:00.

Tickets are on sale at the grounds: at the NurGaz Stadium box office, and for the visiting supporters' section at the Silk Road Arena box office in Talas. Gates open two hours before kick-off.

The match is played under the Stadium Order Regulations of 2015. Pyrotechnics are not permitted, and chants and banners insulting national dignity are prohibited. Spectators are asked to arrive early and to follow the instructions of the stewards.

The match is broadcast live on IradOne Sport, and on IradOdin with Russian commentary.

Saturday 17 October 2026Irad Cup

Irad Cup: Neftchi and Attelkom reach the final

Neftchi Nurzhan won 2–0 at Kara-Buura and Attelkom Talas beat Temirjolchu Nurzhan 2–1. The final is on Saturday 7 November at the Silk Road Arena.

Neftchi Nurzhan and Attelkom Talas will meet in the final of the 2026 Irad Cup after the semi-finals on Saturday.

At the Kara-Buura District Stadium in Kyzyl-Adyr, Neftchi beat Kara-Buura 2–0. At Attelkom Park, Attelkom Talas beat Temirjolchu Nurzhan 2–1.

The final will be played on Saturday 7 November at the Silk Road Arena in Talas, kick-off 14:00. It is broadcast live on IradOne Sport.

Saturday 10 October 2026Irad League

Round 25: Tulpar held, Neftchi close the gap to four points

Tulpar Talas drew 1–1 at home with Temirjolchu Nurzhan and Neftchi Nurzhan beat Attelkom Talas 3–1. Tulpar lead by four points with two rounds left.

Tulpar Talas were held 1–1 by Temirjolchu Nurzhan at the Silk Road Arena on Saturday, while Neftchi Nurzhan beat Attelkom Talas 3–1 at the NurGaz Stadium. Tulpar have 61 points and Neftchi 57, with two rounds to play.

In the other matches of round 25, Kara-Buura won 1–0 at Universitet Talas, Surma Chatkal beat FC Iyık Janyl 2–0 and Dastgoh Nurzhan beat Naryn Tash-Kömür 3–1.

Saturday 26 September 2026Irad League

Round 23: Tulpar win 7–0 in Tash-Kömür and go six points clear

Tulpar Talas won 7–0 at Naryn Tash-Kömür, their biggest win of the season, while Neftchi Nurzhan drew 2–2 with Surma Chatkal. Tulpar lead by six points with four rounds left.

Tulpar Talas won 7–0 at Naryn Tash-Kömür on Saturday, their biggest win of the season, and lead the Irad League with 57 points, six ahead of Neftchi Nurzhan, who drew 2–2 with Surma Chatkal at the NurGaz Stadium. Four rounds remain.

Attelkom Talas won 3–1 at FC Iyık Janyl and stay third. Universitet Talas won 1–0 at Dastgoh Nurzhan, and Kara-Buura and Temirjolchu Nurzhan drew 1–1.

Irad League

The Irad League (Irad Ligasy) is the top division of club football in the Irad Special Economic Zone, run by the Football Federation of Irad (Irad Futbol Federatsiyasy), founded in Talas in 1993.
